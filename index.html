About Al Ghurair Group

A Legacy Since 1960

The Journey

Company Evolution

From fashion origins to beauty leadership and luxury horizons

Early 2010s
Phase 1: Fashion Foundation
American Vintage Partnership

BIAS Retail established its market presence through an exclusive partnership with American Vintage, the French contemporary basics brand. Stores launched at premium locations including Dubai Mall, Dubai Marina Mall, and City Walk, introducing elevated casual wear to the UAE market [^19^][^20^].

Mid 2010s
Phase 2: Beauty Transition
The Face Shop Expansion

Strategic pivot into beauty retail with The Face Shop, Korea's leading natural skincare brand. Expanded to multiple UAE locations including Mall of the Emirates, Ibn Battuta Mall, BurJuman Centre, and Al Ghurair Centre [^23^][^28^].

2024+
Phase 3: Luxury Lifestyle
Alain Ducasse & Premium F&B

Entering the luxury lifestyle segment with Alain Ducasse retail concepts, signaling a bold move into premium F&B and experiential retail. This represents BIAS Retail's evolution toward curated luxury experiences beyond traditional product retail.

Evolution Timeline Fashion → Beauty → Luxury Experience
